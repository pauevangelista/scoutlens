# Runway standards research (2026-10-15)

What I found, with source quality. None of this replaces checking each agency's own page and a scout's confirmation.

## Height and age are the numbers agencies publish
- Elite UK open casting event: minimum 5'6" (female-presenting category) and 5'11" (male-presenting category), ages 14-20. Applicants who identify as female or non-binary can choose the female-presenting category; male or non-binary applicants the male-presenting one. (event page)
- Elite World Group listing: women 5'8" and taller, men 6' and over, represents ages 18+. (Backstage listing, date unknown)
- Elite Model Look competition: 1.72 m female, 1.88 m male. (encyclopedia)
- MINOIRE (Spain): 1.75 m women, 1.85 m men. (press article, 2022)
- Uno Models director: normal from 1.75 m for a model, with exceptions. (interview)
- Height and age requirements shift from year to year and by division, so every record needs a "last verified" date.

## Bust, waist and hips: rarely published as thresholds
- The numeric rules I found (for example "waist about 10 inches smaller than chest and hips") are rules of thumb from magazines and blogs, not agency requirements. Do not encode them.
- Agencies ask for measurements in applications, but eligibility thresholds are mostly height and age.
- Add a measurement range to a record only when the agency publishes it or a scout confirms it in writing, with the source.

## Spanish agencies mostly do not publish requirements
- Searches for Francina, Uno and others returned directories and blog posts, not requirement pages. Fill these from each agency's official site and your scouts.

## Why weight and BMI stay out
- Spain barred models with BMI under 18 from Madrid Fashion Week in 2006. France requires a doctor's note on BMI and general health for models. The app collects no weight and computes no BMI.

## Facial symmetry (research only)
- The evidence that symmetry drives attractiveness is mixed, and symmetry measured from photos is affected by pose and camera (Weiss et al., 2020, Personality and Individual Differences). Kleisner et al. (2024) found no independent effect in 1,550 natural faces.
- Use: an offline research test of whether scouts' ratings track measured symmetry, never shown to applicants, only with explicit consent and ethics approval.
