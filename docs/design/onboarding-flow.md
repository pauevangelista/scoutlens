# Onboarding flow

| # | Screen | Collects | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome | nothing | Promise: "Show them your best frame." |
| 2 | Scouts told us | nothing | Explains the problem: bad light and weak photos. Claim must match the interview data |
| 3 | Notes, not a score | nothing | Explains the feedback format |
| 4 | Where to apply | nothing | Explains agency matching and why |
| 5 | Consent | 18+, device-only photos, "feedback not judgment" | Gate. Nothing proceeds without all three |
| 6 | Your aim | runway / editorial / commercial / not sure | Orders agencies. Never hides any |
| 7 | Experience | new / some work / signed before | Separates open calls from portfolio submissions |
| 8 | Your details | height, age, city or country; optional chest/bust, waist, hips, shoe size | Agency matching (height, age, market) and the model card. Bust, waist and hips are never compared with a range unless an agency publishes it and the user opts in. No weight, no BMI |
| 9 | Your digitals | face close-up, full length, side profile | Photo feedback checks |
| 10 | Reading your photos | nothing | Short moment while checks run on the device |
| 11 | Your notes | nothing | Status, reason and tip per check. No score |
| 12 | Where to look | division chosen by the user, per the agency's own categories | Matches and non-matches, each explained, with source and last-verified date |

Optional fairness questions (gender, age band) are separate. They are only used to test that feedback is identical across groups and never change what anyone sees.

Rules: photo feedback never uses gender, age, experience or skin tone. Agency matching uses the user's declared division, age, height and experience only against the agency's own published criteria.
