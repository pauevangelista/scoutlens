
## Design
- Look: modern and sleek. See docs/design/design-system.md for tokens. Do not invent new colors, radii or fonts.
- Flow: follow docs/design/onboarding-flow.md. The design canvas source is in docs/design/canvas/.
- No score anywhere in the UI. Statuses are always a word plus an icon.
- Photo placeholders are labelled P1 to P6. Use docs/design/photo-shot-list.md. Never use real people's photos in the repo.
- Motion only where the design shows it, and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
